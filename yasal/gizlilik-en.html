2. Short version

Fincan asks for no account, creates no identifier for you, and uses no advertising network or analytics tool.

Your photograph is processed on your device. When the AI-assisted reading is active, only the image cropped to the inside of the cup is sent to the service provider for the duration of the reading, and we do not store it.

Entering your name is optional and it stays on your device.

3. Data we process

a) Cup photograph: the image you upload or capture in the app. Before interpretation we crop it to the ring you drew; everything outside the cup (handle, saucer, table, your hand, background) is painted black and is not processed.

b) Name (optional): used to address you during the reading. It is never sent to a server; it is kept in your device's local storage and is removed when you delete the app.

c) Preferences: settings such as language choice are stored on your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or similar data.

4. Purpose and legal basis

Your data is processed solely to produce the entertainment reading you requested.

Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for, and your consent given when you upload a photograph.

Your photograph is not used for profiling, ad targeting, model training or marketing.

5. International transfer

When the AI-assisted reading is active, the image cropped to the inside of the cup is transferred to servers of an AI service provider located outside your country in order to generate the reading. This transfer relies on your explicit consent.

The transferred image is used only for that reading and is not recorded by us. The provider's own retention and processing policies apply.

When the AI reading is off, no image leaves your device and the reading is produced entirely on the device.

6. Retention

We keep no photograph on any server of ours. The image held inside the app lives only in the device's temporary memory until you close the app or start a new reading.

Your name and language preference remain on your device until you clear them or remove the app.

7. Your rights

You may ask whether your personal data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, learn the third parties it is transferred to at home or abroad, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request erasure or destruction, request that such actions be notified to third parties, object to a result reached solely by automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage suffered.

Send requests to info@innowhatif.com. We answer within thirty days at the latest.

Because there is no account and no photograph is retained, an erasure request is in most cases satisfied simply by removing the app from your device.

8. Children

The app is not directed at users under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from that age group.

9. Security

Where a transfer occurs, data is sent using current encryption standards (HTTPS/TLS). Even so, no method of transmission is entirely secure.

Please make sure your cup photograph contains no other person's face, identity document or sensitive data.

10. Changes

This text may be updated. The update date appears at the top, and material changes are announced inside the app.
